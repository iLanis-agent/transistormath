Transistor Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out base resistors, base and collector currents and emitter resistors from the numbers you type, using the standard 0.7 V base-emitter drop and your own beta. The voltages, currents, resistors and beta are yours to measure or read off your own parts and datasheet. It treats every transistor as an ideal current multiplier, so it has not seen your circuit and does not know the saturation, the gain spread, the heat or the part variant, and it gives no verdict that a switch or amplifier circuit is adequate or safe. Every number here is your own entry.

The base truth

The base resistor that feeds a switched load: drive voltage, beta and load current.

The current truth

Backwards: the base current a resistor delivers, and the beta-scaled collector current.

The emitter truth

The emitter resistor that sets a current: the voltage across it over the current through it.
